The primary hydrographic registry numbers mapping the Straits of Mackinac area for .bag (Bathymetric Attributed Grid) data files fall under NOAA's 2019 Straits of Mackinac survey project (Project OPR-X388-KR-19).

The exact survey registry numbers to locate or download these .bag files from the NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) Bathymetric Data Viewer are:

Core Straits of Mackinac Survey Numbers
- H13252: Covers the West Straits of Mackinac, mapping roughly 40 square nautical miles.
- H13253: Covers the central/main passageways of the Lake Huron/Lake Michigan boundary lines.
- H13255: Maps the East Straits of Mackinac and the vicinity of Grays Reef Passage.
- H13257: Covers the nearshore areas surrounding Mackinac Island, Round Island, and Bois Blanc Island.
- H13258: Continues adjoining coverage through the primary shipping channels.
- H13259: Maps northern territory sections approximately 3 nautical miles North of Garden Island.
- H13256: Focuses nearby in the vicinity of Ile Aux Galets.

How to Access the .bag Files
1. Go to the NOAA NCEI Bathymetric Data Viewer.
2. Search by entering any of the Registry Numbers listed above (e.g., H13252).
3. Download the specific survey package containing the gridded .bag files or its accompanying text-based Descriptive Report (DR).

References cited in thread: Office of Coast Survey (.gov), ArcGIS Online, NCEI (.gov) — NOAA releases 2019 hydrographic survey plans; H13255, H13257 survey metadata pages.
