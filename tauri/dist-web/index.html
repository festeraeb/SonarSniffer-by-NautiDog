CESARops is an open-source maritime search and rescue platform built after the Coast Guard would not run back-drift analysis on evidence from the Rosa — Charlie Brown case. Shipwreck hunting is the art; bringing people home is the mission.

What we build

Drift & satellite stack

Rust/Vulkan engine with nauticuvs filters — sediment plume subtraction, thermal shimmer detection, navigation-grade coordinate fusion when VRAM allows full-stack runs.

Triple-lock detection

Scout, validator, and jitter workers cross-check tiles before Forge orchestrates the next move. Cluster degrades gracefully when augment nodes drop offline.

Great Lakes STEM

Youth-built drifters broadcasting live telemetry into the validation stack — hands-on engineering feeding real current data for Line 5 and SAR missions.

Always free for SAR

Commercial lanes (SonarSniffer, industrial monitoring) fund compute. The wreck hunt and missing-person stack stays open and community-led.
