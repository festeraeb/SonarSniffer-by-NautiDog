# Satellite Data Educational Grant Request

**Subject:** Educational/Non-Profit Data Access Request: AI-Powered Search & Rescue and STEM Education

**To:** Maxar Grant / Educational Programs Team

Dear **Maxar** Team,

My name is **Thomas**, and I am a substitute teacher dedicated to getting students excited about Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). I am writing to you today to request educational or research access to your commercial satellite imagery and APIs for an ambitious, student-inspiring project called **CesarOps / WreckHunter**.

We have built a distributed, multi-GPU AI processing pipeline that streams and analyzes satellite imagery in near real-time. Our mission is three-fold:

1. **STEM Education:** To show students the incredible power of artificial intelligence, orbital mechanics, and remote sensing. By interacting with live data, students see firsthand how computer science and aerospace technology can solve real-world problems.
2. **Search and Rescue (SAR):** We are designing this system to detect anomalies (such as lost vessels, environmental hazards, or thermal signatures). We are providing this analysis and platform to active Search and Rescue groups **completely free of charge**.
3. **Cultural Preservation:** We use multi-spectral analysis to locate, identify, and document historical shipwrecks, aiding in the preservation of underwater cultural resources.

Currently, we rely entirely on public datasets like Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-1/2 via the Microsoft Planetary Computer. While this is an excellent starting point for the students, the spatial resolution and revisit times of public satellites limit our operational effectiveness, particularly for live Search and Rescue missions where time is critical. 

Because we are entirely self-funded on a teacher's budget—we literally build our compute nodes from heavily modified, repurposed hardware—we cannot afford commercial licensing rates. 

We are asking if **Maxar** would be willing to partner with us by granting educational, non-profit, or research tier access to your data archives and APIs. Doing so would not only drastically improve a system built to save lives and preserve history, but it will also give the next generation of engineers and scientists hands-on experience with your organization's cutting-edge capabilities.

I would love to arrange a brief call or provide a demonstration of our current AI inference pipeline to show you exactly how your data would be put to good use. 

Thank you for your time, and for your commitment to advancing both technology and education.
